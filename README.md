# Customer Churn Prediction

Customer retention is one of the most important challenges businesses face. Acquiring new customers is often more expensive than retaining existing ones, making churn prediction a valuable business problem.

In this project, I build a machine learning model to predict whether a customer is likely to leave a company based on factors such as demographics, account information, service usage, and customer behavior.

The project follows a complete data science workflow, including:

* Business understanding
* Data exploration and analysis
* Data cleaning and preprocessing
* Feature engineering
* Model training and evaluation
* Business insights and recommendations

The goal is not only to build an accurate predictive model but also to identify the factors that contribute most to customer churn and provide actionable recommendations for improving customer retention.
